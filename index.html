A cheerful neighborhood milk tea shop made to bring refreshing flavors, affordable treats, and happy moments to students, workers, families, and every milk tea lover in Cebu.



ABOUT US

XmarynJoy's Milktea Shop is an affordable, homegrown Cebuano beverage chain founded in 2026 by Jessie Rey Duallo. Our shop was created with a simple goal: to provide delicious, refreshing, and budget-friendly milk tea that everyone can enjoy. We believe that a great drink does not have to be expensive, which is why our drinks and snacks start at an affordable base price of ₱39.

At XmarynJoy's, every cup is prepared with care, creativity, and a little bit of joy. Our menu includes creamy, sweet, refreshing, and exciting flavors that are perfect for any mood. Whether you are grabbing a drink from school, relaxing after work, spending time with friends, or enjoying a family bonding moment, we have something special waiting for you.

Our mission is to make every customer feel welcome and satisfied. We want XmarynJoy's Milktea Shop to become a friendly place where people can enjoy good drinks, delicious snacks, and memorable moments without spending too much.
